I’m a Member of Technical Staff at Mantic where I work on post-training. We are on a mission to solve forecasting and radically improve human decision making.

I briefly worked on sequential Bayesian experimental design research at OATML during my visiting student year at Oxford. I am currently on a leave of absence from Brown where I study Engineering Physics.

I’m interested in reinforcement learning, Bayesian inference, decision making under uncertainty, and AI for science.

I grew up in Melbourne, Australia and am currently based in London, UK. I love travelling, music, cricket and learning more about physics, philosophy, history and politics.
